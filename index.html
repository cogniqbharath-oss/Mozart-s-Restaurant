Our Story

A Legacy of Culinary Excellence

Nestled in the heart of Leavenworth, Mozart's Restaurant has been serving exceptional European and Pacific Northwest cuisine for over four decades. Our woman-owned establishment combines the rich traditions of Austrian cooking with innovative modern techniques.

Located upstairs at 829 Front Street, our intimate dining room offers breathtaking views and a romantic atmosphere perfect for special occasions. From our extensive wine cellar to our Friday night live music performances, every detail is crafted for an unforgettable experience.
